# Questions for John

If time becomes limited, prioritize these:

## Litigation Judgment

- What usually wins cases?
- What usually loses cases?
- When do you know you have a strong case?
- When do you know you have a weak case?
- What scares you most in litigation?

---

## Depositions

- What answer immediately gets your attention?
- What answer do you save for trial?
- What answer makes you think "I can impeach this witness"?
- What answer makes you think "This witness is dangerous"?
- What deposition mistakes do lawyers commonly make?

---

## Motion Practice

- What fact changes everything?
- What do judges care about most?
- What do lawyers overvalue?
- What do lawyers overlook?
- Why do motions fail?

---

## New Lawyers

- What do inexperienced lawyers miss?
- What should every litigator know?
- What habits separate good litigators from average ones?
- What habits destroy cases?
- What would you teach a first-year associate?

---

## Product Validation

- If Legal AI could only help with one thing, what would it be?
- What output would save you the most time?
- What output would you trust?
- What output would you ignore?
- What would make this indispensable?
