# Matter Evaluation Rubric

If Legal AI analyzes a matter today, can it answer:

## Matter Understanding

□ What probably happened?

□ Who are the parties?

□ Who are the important witnesses?

□ What documents matter most?

□ What is the procedural posture?

□ What deadlines or upcoming events matter?

---

## Disputed Facts

□ What facts are disputed?

□ Who disputes them?

□ What evidence supports each side?

---

## Verification

□ What facts require client verification?

□ Which conclusions are supported by documents?

□ Can every important conclusion be verified?

---

## Responsibility

□ Who controlled what?

□ Who made decisions?

□ Who had notice?

---

## Credibility

□ Are there major credibility problems?

□ Are there impeachment opportunities?

□ Are there changing stories?

---

## Open Questions

□ What do we still not know?

□ What additional information is needed?

---

## Practical Test

At the end of the report, would a new lawyer be able to understand the matter and safely begin working on it?
