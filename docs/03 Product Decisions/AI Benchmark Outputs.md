
# AI Benchmark Outputs

## Test Matter

Marine Bulkheading v. JAT Construction

Task:
Draft affirmation in opposition + memorandum of law

## Compared Models

- Claude
- Gemini
- ChatGPT (pending)
- Westlaw AI (pending)
- Lexis (pending)

## Early Result

Claude performed strongest.

Best strengths:
- stronger procedural framing
- stronger CPLR 3211 handling
- better lien fund analysis
- stronger legal roadmap structure

Gemini was usable but lighter.

## Strategic Rule

Legal AI should be judged against:
usable litigation output

not
case summaries alone.
