# Matter Builder v2.8 — Citation + Exhibit Engine

## What We Built

- Citation + Exhibit Engine added
    
- Exhibit detection
    
- Affidavit / affirmation support extraction
    
- Fact → document mapping
    
- Suggested citation language (Exhibit A, NYSCEF Doc. No. __)
    
- Contradiction panel (structure in place)
    

## Key Insight (Attorney Feedback)

The product MUST identify:

→ What actually matters in the case  
→ What wins or loses the motion

Failure of Westlaw/Lexis:

- Missed core issue (e.g., Longardo declaration)
    
- Too generic (Westlaw)
    
- Too verbose / unfocused (Lexis)
    

## Product Direction Shift

From:

- drafting assistant  
    To:
    
- litigation attack engine
    

## What Matters Most

- factual similarity > exact holding
    
- outcome matters
    
- court hierarchy matters (COA > Dept > others)
    
- attorneys bend facts + holdings strategically
    

## Drafting Preference

- wants FULL draft (not outline)
    
- affirmation/opposition style
    
- research = drafting
    

## v3 Plan — Core Issue Detection Engine

Build:

- primary issue detection
    
- contradiction detection (complaint vs affidavit vs exhibit)
    
- affidavit inconsistency detection
    
- unsupported claim detection
    
- missing proof detection
    
- timeline conflicts
    
- “heart of motion” identification
    

## Example Outputs (Target)

- “Declaration contradicts complaint timeline”
    
- “Damages unsupported by invoices”
    
- “No documentary proof of notice”
    
- “Affidavit conflicts with prior statement”
    

## Why This Matters

This becomes:  
→ the core differentiation  
→ the “lawyer brain” layer

Everything else (drafting, citations) sits on top of this.
